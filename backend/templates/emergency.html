What to Do in an Emergency

Follow these steps when you need emergency care

Immediate Steps

  1. Call emergency number immediately
  2. Stay calm and provide clear information
  3. Follow dispatcher instructions
  4. Do not move the patient unless necessary
  5. Keep patient warm and comfortable

When to Call Emergency

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Severe bleeding
  • Sudden severe headache
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe burns or injuries
  • Poisoning or overdose
